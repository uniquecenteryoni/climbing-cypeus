Cedar Valley Trail

3-4 hours Easy-Moderate Groups up to 15 people 6 km

Overview

Cedar Valley is one of Cyprus's hidden natural treasures. The trail passes through an ancient and rare cedar forest, where Cyprian cedars (Cedrus Brevifolia) grow - an endemic cedar species found only in Cyprus.

The path leads through spectacular mountain landscapes, deep valleys and dense cedar forests. The trail offers a perfect combination of wilderness, unique vegetation, breathtaking views and historical experience - the cedar trees have been part of the local culture and economy for thousands of years.

Important Information

Recommended season: Spring and Autumn (March-May, September-November)

Difficulty level: Easy-Moderate. Suitable for hikers with basic fitness

What to bring: Closed comfortable shoes, hat, sunscreen, water (at least 1.5 liters), energy snack

Recommended clothing: Comfortable breathable clothing, layers (temperature varies in the forest)
