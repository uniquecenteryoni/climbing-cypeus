One-Way Hike to Avakas Canyon

4-5 hours Moderate-Challenging Groups up to 10 people

Overview

Spectacular hike in Avakas Canyon - one of the most impressive canyons in Europe! This canyon is similar to the famous canyons in Jordan and Arizona in the USA, and it also appeared in the famous movie "127 Hours". The experience includes walking between high and narrow walls, in water and on rocks, with breathtaking views.

The canyon is located in the Akamas Peninsula in northwest Cyprus, and it offers a physical challenge combined with stunning natural beauty. The trail is suitable for hikers with reasonable fitness and includes climbing on large rocks, walking in water and narrow passages between the high rock walls.

Tour Highlights

  • Spectacular canyon with walls up to 30 meters high
  • Walking in water and on large rocks
  • Unique views of amazing geology
  • Challenging and exciting experience
  • Unique desert flora and fauna
  • Out and back trail
