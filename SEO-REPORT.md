## Search Console checks still required

These cannot be completed from the repository without access to the property in Google Search Console:

1. Inspect `/`, `/activities`, `/course`, `/bouldering-cyprus` and their English equivalents.
2. Confirm that the sitemap was read successfully and has no submitted-versus-indexed gap.
3. Export the last 90 days of queries and group them into branded, climbing, lead-course, rappelling and bouldering queries.
4. Review pages in positions 11–30 first; they are the fastest candidates for title, internal-link and content improvements.
5. Check `Crawled - currently not indexed`, duplicate canonical, and alternate-page errors.
6. Compare impressions by country and device. Local results vary by searcher location, so one manual search is not a reliable rank measurement.
