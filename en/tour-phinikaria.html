Phinikaria Mountain Village Tour

3-4 hours Easy Groups up to 12 people 5 km

Overview

Phinikaria village is one of the most charming mountain villages in the Troodos area. The village sits at 900 meters above sea level and offers stunning views, authentic Cypriot atmosphere, and traditional stone architecture.

The tour includes a village tour, walking on the natural trails around, getting to know the local culture, and visiting a small traditional restaurant. The experience combines nature and culture and is suitable for those seeking an authentic and peaceful experience in the heart of the mountains.

Tour Highlights

  • Tour of traditional mountain village
  • Stunning views of Troodos mountains
  • Traditional stone architecture
  • Walking on natural trails
  • Meeting authentic Cypriot culture
  • Local food tasting
