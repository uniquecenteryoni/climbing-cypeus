One-Way Hike to the Arches Trail

3-4 hours Easy-Moderate Groups up to 12 people

Overview

If you were looking for an experience of a charming stream with playful rocks like in the movies, pools with water flowing underneath - this is your trail! The Arches Trail is one of the most beautiful and special trails in Cyprus, passing through a stream with flowing water, large rocks, and rich vegetation.

The trail is suitable for the whole family and offers a unique experience of walking in the stream, immersion in local nature, and enjoying the peace and beauty of the natural environment. The tour also includes explanation about local flora and fauna, and the history of the area.

Tour Highlights

  • Walking in stream beside flowing water
  • Rich and diverse vegetation
  • Suitable for the whole family
  • Recommended season: all year round
