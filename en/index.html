I shape each climbing day around your people, experience, and the area that suits you best. Whether it is your first time on rock or you are experienced climbers looking for a different side of Cyprus.

Hi, I'm Yonatan, originally from Israel, now living in Cyprus. I am a certified rock climbing and rappelling guide with extensive experience leading tours and courses.

My love for nature and adventure led me to create Climbing Cyprus, a guided experience that helps visitors discover the island’s landscapes and climbing areas.

I specialize in nature hikes, rock climbing and rappelling, providing professional and safe guidance for all levels - from beginners to advanced.

"Every climbing day is built around the people, their experience, and the adventure that suits them."
