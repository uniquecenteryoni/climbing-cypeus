Bouldering in Cyprus

Cyprus has outdoor bouldering around Akamas, Ineia and Droushia, with hundreds of problems, open views and approaches suitable for short sessions or a full climbing day.

What can we help with?

  • Local area suggestions and problems suited to your level.
  • A local guidebook with maps, GPS pins and approach notes.
  • Crash pad and selected gear rental, subject to availability.
  • Advice about season, weather, access and outdoor safety.

Who is it for?

Independent climbers, couples and groups visiting Cyprus who want to climb outdoors. Always check heat, access, rock condition and landing zones before climbing; this page does not replace judgement and preparation at the crag.
