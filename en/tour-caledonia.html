Caledonia Waterfall Trail

2-3 hours Easy Groups up to 15 people 3 km

Overview

The Caledonia trail is one of the most beautiful and accessible trails in the Troodos Mountains. The trail follows the Kryos Potamos stream through a dense pine forest and leads to the spectacular Caledonia waterfall, approximately 12 meters high.

The path is suitable for the whole family and is well-maintained with clear signage. The trail offers shade for most of the way, soothing sounds of flowing water, and lovely forest views. During the rainy and spring seasons, the waterfall is at its fullest and the experience is particularly spectacular.

Tour Highlights

  • Spectacular Caledonia waterfall (12 meters)
  • Dense and refreshing pine forest
  • Kryos stream with flowing water
  • Well-maintained and clearly marked trail
  • Suitable for families with children
  • Shade throughout the entire trail
